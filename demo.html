Pixel World — Live Demo

An infinite caveman world that paints its own art — now with a campfire to share.

🔥 Enter the world

You spawn beside the camp with your own caveman. Everyone who opens this page appears in your world — walk up, say hi, and watch the words pop above their heads.

👥

Play together

Every visitor controls their own caveman near the fire. Other players' humans appear as live ghosts with name tags — no accounts, no servers of ours.

💬

Chat bubbles

Type a message and it pops as a pixel bubble above your human — and above the human of whoever you're talking to.

🧙

Grunk the Elder

A knowledge NPC by the fire answers questions about the world: seasons, fish, trees, fire, sleep and more. Ask him for help.

🗺️

The full world

Four biomes, 25 painted species, 6 fish, day & night, seasons, knowledge book, Move commands and carry mechanics — everything the game has.

Multiplayer chat rides a public MQTT demo broker over WebSockets (HiveMQ/EMQX) — zero sign-ups, but it's a shared public channel, so keep it friendly. If the broker is unreachable the camp still works solo and Grunk still chats with you.
Tip: you can always come back — index.html?demo=1 is the demo entry point.
